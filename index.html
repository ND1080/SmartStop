Travel Information & Passenger Assistance

Station amenities, safety protocols, wheelchair access, lost property, and emergency contacts in Coimbatore

Coimbatore Smart City Transport Support
RULE 1

Platform Yellow Line Safety

Always stand behind the tactile yellow safety border until the arriving bus comes to a complete halt and doors are opened by the conductor.

RULE 2

Boarding & Alighting Queue

Maintain orderly queues at designated bay markers. Let arriving passengers alight completely before boarding.

MONITORING

24/7 CCTV Surveillance

Gandhipuram bus terminal is equipped with 20 high-definition security cameras monitored round the clock by Coimbatore City Police.

EMERGENCY

Emergency Contact Helplines

Police & Medical SOS: 112 / 100
Women Safety Helpline: 1091
TNSTC Grievance / Control: 0422-2433055
Station Master Gandhipuram: +91 422 252 3011
ACCESSIBLE BAYS

Wheelchair Ramps & Low-Floor Bays

Bay 1 and Bay 4 feature zero-step boarding ramps designed specifically for electric and manual wheelchairs connecting directly to low-floor AC Volvo and deluxe buses.

TACTILE GUIDANCE

Tactile Paving for Visually Impaired

Continuous bright yellow guiding tiles lead from the main Cross Cut Road pedestrian entrance directly to all 6 bus boarding platforms.

PRIORITY

Reserved Priority Seating

The front 8 seats in every public bus are legally reserved for senior citizens, expectant mothers, and passengers with disabilities. Co-passengers must kindly vacate upon request.

AUDIO SUPPORT

Bilingual Audio Assistance

This kiosk includes a high-contrast mode, adjustable font sizing (A/A+), and an automatic audio announcement button to read out arriving bus details aloud.

LOCATION

Station Property Office

The Gandhipuram Lost & Found Counter is situated at Bay 2, Ground Floor (next to the Station Controller's cabin). Open 24 hours daily.

PROCEDURE

How to Claim Misplaced Belongings

Present a valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, or Driving License) along with details of the bus number, approximate travel time, and item description.

DIRECT HELPLINE

Lost Property Helpline

Call our designated property assistance desk at +91 422 252 3012 or email lostfound.cbe@smartstop.demo.tn.gov.in.

RETENTION POLICY

Safe Custody Retention

Valuable documents, bags, and electronic items are securely cataloged and held for 30 days before being transferred to the Government District Treasury.

ROUTE DIVERSION Updated Today, 14:15 IST

Avinashi Road Elevated Corridor Diversion

Due to flyover expansion work near Lakshmi Mills & Nava India, Airport Route 1C operates with a minor 8-minute diversion via Kamarajar Road.

NIGHT SERVICE Permanent Schedule

All-Night Feeder Buses (22:30 to 05:00)

Hourly midnight feeder services operate connecting Gandhipuram to Coimbatore Junction Railway Station, Singanallur, and Ukkadam throughout the night.

TEMPLE SPECIAL Pilgrim Mobility

High-Frequency Marudhamalai & Perur Specials

Direct express non-stop services to Marudhamalai Murugan Temple (Route 70) and Perur Pateeswarar Temple (Route 20A) depart every 8 minutes from Bay 1 & Bay 3.
